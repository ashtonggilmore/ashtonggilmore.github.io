## From a first model to a first print

I developed these guides for Southern Utah University’s Department of Engineering & Technology in collaboration with BZI Steel. The goal was to take students from no prior experience to modeling and printing their own projects over one week.

## Two starting points

The middle school materials use TinkerCAD, accessible language, and visual examples. The high school materials go further into design decisions, tolerances, and iteration.

I wrote the curriculum, made the illustrations, and organized the slides and quick-start guides. I also taught the class with support from a teaching aide with CAD experience.

## Materials to use and revisit

The finished PDFs provide a resource for the classroom and a reference students can return to while working. Both versions are available here, so the work can be evaluated directly.
