## Solving a shop problem

I made this stand to hold the ISO30 tapers and the bearing used during tool changes on the shop router. It took roughly 90 minutes to design, fabricate, and install.

The useful part of this project was its scope: a small fixture made to solve an immediate problem at the machine.
