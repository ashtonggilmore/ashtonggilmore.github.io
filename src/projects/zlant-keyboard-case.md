## Building around an existing keyboard

I drew the case in CorelDraw X7 from sketches by Colton Burnside. The stacked acrylic plates are held together with M2 brass standoffs. I also hand-soldered the Kailh hotswap sockets.

This was a combination of enclosure fabrication and electronics assembly.
