## Making room for six workstations

I built the table from 2×4 lumber to keep material costs down. It provides space for six CAD computers, with custom monitor mounts, power and Ethernet routing, stool-height seating, and footrests.

The project brought furniture construction and the practical requirements of a shared computer workspace together.
